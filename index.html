My Work with Children, Families and Communities

I have worked with young children and their families for more than eight years. I started as a kindergarten coordinator. I helped run the daily program, supported the educators, and kept families updated on how their children were growing. Next I spent five years in a primary school, where I worked with children every day and stayed in close touch with their families. Most recently I worked in a Montessori classroom. I set up a calm, organized space and guided children through hands-on activities they chose themselves. I watched for each child's interests and built on them. One thing I learned is that children do best when families and educators work as a team. This fall I started the Early Childhood Education program at Humber Polytechnic, and I'm excited to bring my experience into Ontario early years settings.

My Professional Goals

I want to keep creating play-based, inclusive programs where every child feels like they belong. I hope to build strong partnerships with families from many different backgrounds, since every family knows their child best. I also want to bring parts of my Montessori background into my practice, like calm spaces and child-led learning. In the future I would like to become a Registered Early Childhood Educator and work in a community-focused program.
