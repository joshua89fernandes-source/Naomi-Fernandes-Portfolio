Cover Letter & Resumé

Cover Letter

Naomi Nikol Fernandes · Toronto area, Ontario · Naomi91fernandes@gmail.com

Hello,

My name is Naomi Fernandes. I am in my first semester of the Early Childhood Education program at Humber Polytechnic. I have worked with young children and their families for more than eight years.

I started as a kindergarten coordinator. After that I spent five years in a primary school. Most recently I worked in a Montessori classroom, where I set up a calm, organized space and guided children through hands-on activities they chose themselves. I watched for each child's interests and planned around them. In every role I stayed in close touch with families, because I believe children do best when families and educators work as a team.

I am a practical person. I like a clean, well organized room, and I stay calm when plans change. Outside of work I love hiking across Canada and cooking for the people around me.

My goal is to become a Registered Early Childhood Educator and work in a play-based, inclusive program where every child feels like they belong.

Thank you for taking the time to get to know me.

Warm regards,
Naomi Nikol Fernandes
